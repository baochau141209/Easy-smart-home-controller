# Easy-smart-home-controller
I wrote this a long time ago just to make a smart home controller with Blynk easier and sooo cheappp . A good way to start learning about how to use Blynk and Esp32

# Features
- 4-channelrelay controller
- control through Blynk
- Wifi connection
- simple and low-cost hardware
- real-time device control

# Hardware
- 1 Esp32-C3
- 4 12V relays
- 1 12v 30x30x8mm cooling fan
- 4 NPN trans BC547
- 4 res 470R
- 4 res 1k
- 4 LEDs
- 4 diodes
- 1 60x80mm PCB prototype board
- 1 caps 100uf 16v
- 1 MP1854 stepdown module
- 2 1x8 female header
- 1 2-pin terminal 3.5mm
- 1 8-pin terminal 3.5mm
